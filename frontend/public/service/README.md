# Service page band photography

Both files are **placeholders**. They are real photographs, not illustrations,
but they are stock images of somebody else's campus and office — replace them
with LeSuccess's own before launch. Nothing else needs to change when you do:
keep the filenames, or update `BAND_IMAGES` in `src/pages/Services/ServicePage.jsx`.

CC0 means public-domain dedication: commercial use is allowed and no
attribution is required. This table is a record for whoever swaps them out,
not a legal obligation.

Both are ~1600px wide and 400–600 KB. They sit under a navy overlay at 60–80%,
so they read as atmosphere rather than detail — if you replace them, a darker,
less busy shot will hold up better than a bright one.
